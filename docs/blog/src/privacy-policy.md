# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

## Who We Are

This blog (`opensass.org`) is operated by Mahmoud Harmouch. We publish technical articles on Rust, open-source tooling, and related topics.

## What Data We Collect

**We do not collect personal data directly.** This is a static-ish blog with no login, no comments, and no data entry forms.

The following data may be collected by third-party systems:

- **Hosting / CDN**: Server logs may capture anonymized IP addresses and request metadata (user agent, page URL, timestamp) for performance and abuse protection purposes. These are not linked to individuals.
- **Analytics** (if enabled): We may use privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected by these tools.

## Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. A `localStorage` entry may be written to save your light/dark theme preference. This stays on your device and is never sent to any server.

## Third-Party Links

This blog links to GitHub, crates.io, PyPI, npm, dev.to, and other external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

## Content Attribution

This blog occasionally uses AI language models (such as Claude, Gemini, or GPT) to assist in drafting or reviewing content. Human authors are responsible for all published content; all facts are cross-checked.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the blog evolves. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
